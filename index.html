01 / About

Not a networking group. A community.

Power Ladies is for women who come as they are — and come ready to help. No résumé, no title, no stage of life required. Just generosity, curiosity, and the belief that we rise together.

We're a referral-based in-person and online community — because the strength of the room depends on the character of the women in it. You're invited in by a member who's already lived it, or through a warm conversation with your local chapter lead.

From CEOs to film producers, small business owners to first-time job seekers, our village is built on one truth: women helping women is the most powerful community there is.

Alongside our complimentary events, we offer programs through our main sponsors, Rize Credit Union and Barely Canadian — two partners who believe in this community as deeply as we do.

05 / Chapters

Local rooms, global reach.

A chapter is a local Power Ladies room — hosted by a member who opens the door, sets the table, and leads the question that kicks everything off: How can I help you?

Each chapter meets monthly. Chapter leaders work with venues, home hosts, and local sponsors to build a gathering that feels like an invitation, not a mixer. We've grown from a garage in Venice to more than two dozen chapters existing or opening soon across North America and Europe. Don't see your city? That's an opportunity.

08 / FAQ

The quick answers.

Who is this for?+
Women of every industry, stage, and season — as long as you show up with generosity and respect. Founders and first-timers, creatives and career-changers, small business owners and CEOs are all at the table. What you do is secondary to who you are.
How do I join?+
Most members are invited in by an existing Power Lady. If you don't know one yet, reach out through the contact form below or email info@thepowerladies.com — we'll connect you with your local chapter lead.
Do I need to apply?+
There's no formal application. When you're referred, your chapter lead may do a quick "good human" Zoom — a ten-minute hello to make sure we're a mutual fit. That's it. If you do not have a referral, we have a waiting list for those who want to join, which will include a brief interview to ensure alignment with the community.
Is there a membership fee?+
Los Angeles chapters are now paid membership, with a few tiers designed to fit different lifestyles. Reach out to info@thepowerladies.com and we'll walk you through the options that make the most sense for you.
What happens after I reach out?+
You'll hear back from a chapter lead within a few days. We'll get to know each other, invite you to your first event, and welcome you into the private app once you're officially in the circle.
Is this available in my city?+
We have chapters opening soon or existing in:
Los Angeles — Westside Los Angeles — The Valley South Bay Los Angeles — Downtown Laguna Niguel Pasadena Malibu San Diego Sacramento Phoenix / Scottsdale Tucson NYC — Lower East Side Washington, DC Toronto Montreal Lisbon London Zurich Mexico City Temecula San Francisco Seattle Beverly Hills Long Beach Hollywood Westlake Village
Don't see your city? You might be the one to start it. Tell us below.
Are men allowed?+
We love our male Power Allies — they're occasionally invited to specific events, and our brother community, The Allies, holds its own gatherings. Our monthly Power Ladies chapter events, however, are for the women in our community.
