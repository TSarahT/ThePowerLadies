Power Ladies

Community Commitment Agreement

Power Ladies is built on authentic connection, mutual support, and the question: "How can I help you?" Our community thrives when members feel safe sharing their challenges, ideas, and aspirations. This Commitment helps us maintain that trust.

We've created a space where women can be vulnerable, authentic, and supportive without worry. By joining Power Ladies, you're agreeing to uphold these community values:

1

Be Gracious

Show up in gratitude for the chance to help fellow members and receive help in turn. When someone makes an introduction for you, close the loop! Thank them, and let them know how it went. A great referral or a helpful piece of advice in passing – both count. Share these moments at meetings and online, so others feel the spirit of giving wherever Power Ladies are gathered.

2

Protect Our Community

Keep member information private. The member directory, contact information, and details about who's in our community are confidential. Share member information, stories, or experiences only with permission.

3

Use Good Judgment

Honor the room. Conversations, stories, business challenges, and personal shares that happen in Power Ladies meetings or events are confidential. You're welcome to share your own experiences, but respect others' privacy.

4

Respect Each Other's Work

Honor the ideas and opportunities members share with the group. If someone brings a business idea, strategy, or opportunity, ask before acting on it, and give credit where it's due.

5

Show Up For Each Other

We want our members to make genuine connections built on mutual trust and respect. We encourage members to talk about their own work and businesses - it's an important part of creating connections! Just make sure you're here for each other, not just for what you can get. We also ask that you do not use the group to recruit members for competing organizations.

6

Disagree Respectfully

Speaking ill of or slandering another member isn't in keeping with the safe space we're building together. If a dispute comes up between members, we offer mediation to help resolve it fairly. Members found to be in violation of this agreement may be asked to leave the group.

7

Bring Your Authentic Self

This commitment works both ways. When you honor this commitment, you create the safe space that allows YOU to be vulnerable and authentic too. That's where the magic happens.

That's it. Really. We're building something special together, and that requires every member to show up with integrity, discretion, and respect for each other.

Questions? Concerns? Reach out to your chapter leader or email us at info@powerladies.com. We're here to support you.
